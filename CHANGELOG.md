# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Exports that join several videos, like the chapters of a long GoPro recording, no longer lose their last frame.

## 0.2.0

- New look, easier to use on phones and tablets.
- The video in the Template step grows with the window.
- Group widgets to move, hide and reorder them together.
- Grouped widgets can follow the group when it moves, or stay where they are.
- Select several widgets with Ctrl or Shift click.
- Align widgets left, center, right, top, middle or bottom, to their group or to the guides.
- Spread three or more widgets with equal space between them.
- Widgets snap to the guides, to the widgets of their group, or both.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+G groups, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroups, Delete removes, Escape deselects.
- New Lap bar widget, with a tick at each lap or the current lap only.
- Distance bars that showed laps now open as Lap bars.
- Widget backgrounds can have any color, opacity and shape (rounded, rectangle, pill, ellipse, circle, slanted).
- Text in widgets can be aligned left, center or right, and top, middle or bottom.
- Slow disk option for hard drives, to avoid failed exports when the videos and the export share the same disk.
- Option to export to the temporary folder first and move the video when it is done.
- Option to limit the export speed.
- The export shows what is slowing it down: drawing the widgets, or encoding and the disk.
- Exports run at a higher priority, to use the fastest processor cores.
- The computer no longer goes to sleep during an export.
- Choose which graphics card encodes the video: NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, by name.
- Faster exports on NVIDIA graphics cards: the videos are decoded, scaled and combined with the widgets on the card.
- Open the app more than once, for example to keep editing while another copy exports.
- The web app exports the final video too, also on Android phones and tablets, using the device's own hardware.
- In the web app, the video is saved where you choose, or downloaded when done.
- The web app warns when the browser or device cannot export a video, or cannot read or write one of its formats, and says what to do.
